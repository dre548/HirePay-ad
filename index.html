For Merchants

How It Works for Your Business

From your first customer to getting paid — six simple steps.

1
Register Your Business

Sign up and connect your M-Pesa Paybill or Till. All customer payments go straight there — HirePay never holds your money.

2
Create a Customer Contract

Enter the item, price, deposit, and installment plan. The app calculates the full schedule automatically — no maths needed.

3
Customer Verifies Their ID

Every buyer scans their National ID (front & back) in the app. No anonymous customers. You always know who you're dealing with.

4
Customer Pays via STK Push

Buyer taps "Pay Now" in HirePay. M-Pesa prompt fires on their phone. They enter PIN — money goes straight to your Till. Instantly.

5
Dashboard Updates Automatically

Balance drops in real time. Your console shows who paid, who owes, and who is overdue — all in one screen. No notebook. No guessing.

6
Track Their GPS Anytime

Every app open logs the customer's phone location. If a customer goes quiet, click "View Location" — a Google Maps pin shows exactly where they are.

For Buyers

How It Works for You as a Buyer

Start owning that item today — without paying the full amount upfront.

1
Walk Into a HirePay Store

Find a merchant using HirePay. Pick what you want — phone, TV, sofa, fridge — and agree on a payment plan with the owner.

2
Download the App & Verify Your ID

Download HirePay and scan your National ID. Your contract and full payment schedule appear instantly in your dashboard.

3
See Everything in One Place

Your dashboard shows your remaining balance, next due date, and every past payment with a digital receipt. No paper. No disputes.

4
Pay with One Tap via M-Pesa

Tap "Pay Now." M-Pesa prompt appears instantly. Enter your PIN — done. Balance updates on the spot. No queues, no cash, no confusion.

5
🎉 Collect Your Item!

App shows a green "Balance Cleared" screen. Show it to the merchant and walk out with your item. That's it.
